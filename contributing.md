**📐 Sdf File Parser** is the complete English-language guide to parsing SDF (Structure-Data File) chemistry files. Learn the V2000 and V3000 formats, how to extract atoms, bonds, coordinates, and properties, and how to convert SDF to JSON, CSV, or SMILES — with runnable Python and RDKit examples. Built for cheminformatics developers, chemistry students, and data scientists working with molecular data. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.
